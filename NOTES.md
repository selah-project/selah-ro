## Lessons

- **Census regexes collide with Romanian itself**: the
  bracket-English stoplist's "are" is Romanian *are* ("has") —
  ⟨are⟩ is lawful supplied copula; and lowercase "cel veșnic" is
  ordinary Romanian ("the eternal": poporul cel veșnic, El cel
  veșnic in a parenthetical). Both regexes scoped (the "are" entry
  removed; Cel Veșnic case-sensitive, title-form only). The
  Ofèl-class hazard now has three homes: bare-word lists, bracket
  stoplists, and title-calque patterns.
- **Kethib→qere compound surfaces**: the burn emitted two tokens
  wearing the record's editorial notation as surface (ויכל→וכל in
  2 Sam 6:2 — duplicated beside the clean token, shifting the whole
  spine; וירגז→ותרגז in 1 Sam 14:15). Both spines rebuilt on the en
  record. Census scripts scan glosses, not surfaces — the arrow
  class caught these only because grep ran on whole files; future
  chairs' censuses should scan surfaces vs the record too.
- **El collides with the Romanian pronoun *el*** ("he") — inherent
  to the transliteration; the reader must carry it. Noted for the
  rails' next pass.
